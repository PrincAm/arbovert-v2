Overall Content Quality score (44) also weighs in severe topical-coverage thinness (service pages ~140-200 words vs. an 800-word floor) and the missing internal-link bridge between services and proof-of-work, both of which matter more for this site's ranking prospects than E-E-A-T alone.

**Readability:** measured on all 17 realization content blocks (101 sentences): avg 13.5 words/sentence, max 23. No run-ons, clause structure is normal, readable Czech. No readability issues found.

**Templating / duplication (measured, not guessed):** word-level Jaccard similarity between all 17 realization content blocks: mean 0.13, max 0.34, min 0.05 — i.e. **not** literal near-duplicate text (species/location substitution keeps the lexicon genuinely different page to page). The issue is structural: all 17 use the identical 3-paragraph arc (dateline → technique → generic reflective closer), which is exactly the "repetitive structure across pages" marker the Sept 2025 QRG flags for low-effort AI content, independent of literal text overlap. Service-page `longDescription` mean Jaccard: 0.08 (even lower overlap, not a duplication problem).

**AI citation readiness:** Good raw material exists (FAQ cites the actual statute with numeric thresholds; LocalBusiness schema is complete with two addresses, geo, service types, founding date) but is under-leveraged: no `Person`/credential schema for the named arborist, service pages have almost no citable specifics (no numbers, no standards, no process steps), and the one keyword-bearing H1 on the homepage is malformed at the text-node level (Finding 2), which affects how extraction tools (search snippets, AI crawlers, screen readers) parse it.
